# Content System

## Article Format
Articles use MDX.

Example frontmatter:
```yaml
---
title: "Why Doesn't the Moon Fall Into Earth?"
description: "An intuitive explanation of orbital motion."
category: "Space"
tags:
  - gravity
  - orbit
  - moon
publishedAt: "2026-08-08"
author: "Curious Minds"
featured: false
---
```

## Article Structure
Prefer:
1. Strong opening question
2. Short explanation
3. Intuitive mental model
4. Scientific explanation
5. Mathematical explanation when useful
6. Interactive visualization
7. Interesting consequences
8. Common misconceptions
9. Summary
10. Further reading

## Principles
Do not artificially make every article long. Depth should depend on the subject. Every article should answer a clear question.

## Interactive Content
Add an interactive component only when it improves understanding.

## Scientific Accuracy
Never invent scientific facts. Clearly distinguish established science from speculation. Include a Sources and Further Reading section when appropriate.
